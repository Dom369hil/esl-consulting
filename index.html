WHY CHOOSE US

Trusted Local Expertise, Global Best Practices.

At ESL, we believe that effective Environment, Health, Sustainability and Safety (EHSS) management is not just about compliance but it’s about smart planning, responsible development, and protecting what matters most: people, the environment, and long-term economic value.

Here’s why leading mining and extractive companies and operators across Ghana and Africa choose ESL Consulting as their EHSS partner of choice:

01

Deep Knowledge of Regulations

We have extensive experience navigating Ghana’s legal and regulatory frameworks, including:

  • L.I. 2258

    Petroleum (E&P) (Health, Safety & Environment) Regulations, 2017

  • L.I. 2182

    Minerals and Mining (Health, Safety & Technical) Regulations, 2012

  • ACT 490

    Environmental Protection Agency Act, 1994

  • ISO 45001
  • ISO 14001
  • ISO 31000
  • IFC / World Bank

    Requirements / Standards (PS1, PS3, PS4, PS6)

We also ensure your projects align with both local laws and international standards—reducing delays, minimizing risks, and securing approvals faster.

02

Partnership Through Tailored Solutions

We take pride in our rigorous, science-led approach to EHSS consulting, ensuring that every recommendation is backed by sound technical analysis and policy insight.

From oil and gas to mining and infrastructure, our team evaluates the economic, environmental, and social impacts of proposed developments to guide clients toward the most sustainable implementation strategies.

Rather than offering generic solutions, we adopt an iterative and adaptive process, combining research, stakeholder engagement, and technical-economic modeling to deliver tailored, practical outcomes.

With ESL, you get a partner that helps you build resilient, future-ready projects grounded in local understanding and global best practices.
